The character rules

1. Keep limbCurve: 0.78 and sleeveMode: "ink" for the reference style.

2. Use pose or hand IK. Do not draw extra joint circles or detached gloves.

3. Held props use hold: {R: "spear"}. The hand automatically becomes a grip.

4. Set hands: {R: "point", L: "relaxed"} to override the automatic gestures.
